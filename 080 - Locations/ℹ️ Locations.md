
Locations are place where you can link [[ℹ️ People]] or [[ℹ️ Meetings]]  in order archieve a better and centralized data structure. In each location you will find all people and meetings linked but also is a place where you can take notes related with that space.

Locations are grouped in countries here, but is only an example of use. You can modify or duplicate this taxonomy to fulfill your needings.

> Locations can be cities, places or address, what fits better to you.
